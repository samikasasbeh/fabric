What can Fabric do?
  • Read rendered page text and discover a limited list of currently visible controls, or read one selected page element.
  • After inspection and separate exact-action approval in Fabric, click a discovered control or fill a non-sensitive field. This can change application data; a dispatched action does not prove the change was saved.
  • It does not access cookies or password fields. Read text can still contain private information.
  • Saved connections authorize the app’s origin in this browser profile, not a verified account identity. Browser reads need an authorized request in Fabric.

Keep the browser and Fabric running. Checks normally happen every 30 seconds and do not open tabs. Up to three helper-owned execution tabs can be open; unused tabs close after three minutes unless you activate or navigate them. A sleeping computer cannot respond.
